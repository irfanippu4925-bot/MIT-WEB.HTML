04 / DESIGN TIPS

Creative clarity starts with better choices.

01 ✦

How to choose colors

Start with a clear mood, then build a palette around contrast, harmony, and a few consistent accents.

  • Limit your palette to 2–3 primary colors
  • Use contrast for hierarchy and emphasis
  • Test colors on dark and light backgrounds
02 Aa

How to choose fonts

Typography should support the message. Pick a readable body font and a distinctive headline style.

  • Pair one expressive display font with one clean sans serif
  • Use spacing and hierarchy to guide attention
  • Keep readability consistent across devices
03 ▣

Poster design tips

Poster design works best when the main message is obvious in a single glance.

  • Lead with a bold headline and focal image
  • Create rhythm through size and spacing
  • Leave breathing room around key elements
04 ◎

Logo design tips

A strong logo should be memorable, scalable, and recognizable in both digital and print formats.

  • Prioritize simplicity and clarity over detail
  • Ensure it works in black and white
  • Design for flexibility across applications
05 ✧

Branding basics

Branding is more than a logo. It is the emotional and visual system that people remember.

  • Define your tone, voice, and visual direction
  • Keep design systems consistent across touchpoints
  • Build trust through clarity and repetition
